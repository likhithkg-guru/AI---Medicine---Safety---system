About MedSafe AI

MedSafe AI is an intelligent healthcare platform designed to detect fake medicines, analyze medicine details using OCR technology, and improve medicine safety awareness among people.

💊 Fake Detection

Our AI system scans medicine strips and identifies suspicious or fake medicines using OCR text analysis and database verification.

🧠 AI Scanner

The scanner extracts medicine names, expiry dates, and medicine information automatically from uploaded images.

📋 Complaint System

Users can register complaints against suspicious medical shops and maintain a public complaint database.

🔍 Medicine Search

Users can search for medicine information including uses and side effects instantly.

🌍 Healthcare Vision

Our goal is to build a smarter healthcare ecosystem that protects people from counterfeit medicines and improves medicine awareness.

🚀 Future Scope

Future upgrades include barcode verification, AI image matching, cloud database integration, and mobile camera scanning.
